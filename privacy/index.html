Your information,

This policy explains what we collect when you register, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. What we collect

When you register, we collect your email address and, if you choose to provide it, your mobile number. We may also receive basic technical information needed to deliver the website, installer, and product support.

2. How we use it

We use this information to send your installer link and setup instructions, manage trial and product access, provide support, and share Cheatly updates when you have agreed to receive them.

3. Payments

Payment details are handled by the payment providers shown at checkout. Cheatly does not ask for or store your full card or payment account credentials through the registration form.

4. Sharing and retention

We use service providers only where needed to operate delivery, payments, hosting, analytics, and support. We do not sell your personal information. We keep information only for as long as it is needed for these purposes or required by law.

5. Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete registration information, or stop receiving non-essential updates, by contacting the Cheatly support community. Some records may need to be retained for security, payment, or legal reasons.

6. Updates

We may update this policy as Cheatly changes. The latest version will always be available on this page.
