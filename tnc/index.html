early access.

These terms explain how Cheatly early access, email delivery, installer access, support, and responsible use work.

1. Early access

Joining the early-access list records your email address and optional mobile number so we can contact you about Cheatly availability, installer delivery, setup, and support. Joining the list does not guarantee immediate access or a particular release date.

2. Installer delivery

When access is approved, the installer or download link may be sent to the email address submitted through the website. Keep your email address secure and do not share private download links publicly. Delivery timing can vary while we review compatibility and prepare the release.

3. Responsible use

Cheatly is a private workspace for preparation, notes, terminal commands, and permitted AI-assisted workflows. You are responsible for following the rules of your interviewer, employer, client, school, meeting host, and any platform you use. Do not use Cheatly to deceive, impersonate, bypass an assessment rule, or violate confidentiality.

4. Compatibility and support

The current release is intended for supported Apple Silicon Macs and supported macOS versions described on the Cheatly website. Screen-capture behavior can vary by macOS settings and meeting application. Contact the Cheatly community for installation or technical support.

5. Payments and refunds

If a paid release is offered, its price and payment terms will be shown before checkout. Digital products are non-refundable after delivery has started, except where applicable law requires otherwise. Early-access registration itself does not require payment.

6. Privacy and communication

We use the information you submit to manage early access, send installer and product updates, and provide support. We do not ask for passwords, payment card details, or private meeting content through the early-access form. You may request help with your information through the Cheatly support channel.

7. Changes

We may update these terms as Cheatly moves from early access to a paid release. The latest version will always be available at this page.
