Questions, answered

Before you
get started.

Still unsure? Ask in Discord and we’ll help you choose the right setup.

What is Cheatly in one sentence?

Cheatly is a private PTY terminal for macOS that keeps your terminal, notes, and AI tools available while you share a different screen in supported capture setups.

What is Cheatly?

Cheatly is a real PTY-powered terminal overlay for macOS. It gives you a private workspace while you share your screen.

Can I run AI LLM tools like Codex or Claude?

Yes. You can run Codex, Claude, local LLM tools, git, scripts, and other command-line tools available in your Mac terminal.

How do I receive Cheatly?

Join the early-access list with your email. After approval, eligible users receive the installer and setup instructions by email, and the seven-day trial starts after approval.

Which Macs are supported?

The current release supports macOS 13 or later on Apple Silicon Macs.

Who is Cheatly for?

Cheatly is for Mac users who want a focused private workspace for screen sharing, client calls, presentations, and online retrospectives. Interview use is secondary and requires permission.

What shortcuts does Cheatly have?

⌘ M toggles click-through mode, ⌘ N shows or hides the overlay, and standard ⌘ C and ⌘ V copy and paste work in the terminal.

Which screen-sharing apps can I use?

Cheatly is designed for supported capture paths in apps such as Teams, Zoom, Google Meet, and WhatsApp. Capture behavior can vary by app, call mode, and system settings, so test your setup before an important session.

Is Cheatly meant for cheating in interviews?

No. Cheatly is not a hidden interview-answer or cheating tool. Users must follow interviewer, employer, client, school, meeting, and platform rules and obtain permission before using AI tools.

What does joining the list do?

It lets us contact you about the first release. Approval is required before we email the installer and start the seven-day trial.
