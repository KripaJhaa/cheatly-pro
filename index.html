Questions, answered

Before you
get started.

Still unsure? Ask in Discord and we’ll help you choose the right setup.

What is Cheatly?

Cheatly is a real PTY-powered terminal overlay for macOS. It gives you a private workspace while you share your screen.

Can I run AI LLM tools like Codex or Claude?

Yes. You can run Codex, Claude, local LLM tools, git, scripts, and other command-line tools available in your Mac terminal.

How do I receive Cheatly?

Join the early-access list with your email. After approval, the installer and setup instructions will be sent to you by email.

Which Macs are supported?

The current release supports macOS 13 or later on Apple Silicon Macs.

What shortcuts does Cheatly have?

⌘ M toggles click-through mode, ⌘ N shows or hides the overlay, and standard ⌘ C and ⌘ V copy and paste work in the terminal.

Which screen-sharing apps can I use?

Cheatly is designed for supported capture paths in apps such as Teams, Zoom, Google Meet, and WhatsApp. Capture behavior can vary by app, call mode, and system settings, so test your setup before an important session.

Is Cheatly meant for cheating in interviews?

No. Cheatly does not support cheating or deceptive use during interviews, exams, or professional calls. Ask your interviewer or meeting host for permission before using LLM tools, and follow their rules.

Is payment required now?

No. This form only records interest. Pricing and payment details will be shared before a paid release.
